# Official support and scam safety

## How do I contact official support?

Official support is available in two places only: the in-app help widget and the public #support channel. There is no support by email, phone or direct message (DM).

## Will an admin message me first?

No. Admins and team members never send the first direct message (DM). They never ask for your seed phrase or private key. If you were sent a DM by someone who says they are an admin or from support, it is not real support. It is a scam.

## Is there a wallet validation, sync or rectification tool?

No. AcmeSwap has no wallet validation tool, no wallet sync tool and no rectification tool. A website or person that asks you to validate, sync or rectify your wallet is trying to steal your funds.
