# Lost seed phrase

## I lost my seed phrase. Can AcmeSwap recover it?

No. AcmeSwap is non-custodial and never has your seed phrase (also called a recovery phrase), so it cannot recover or reset it. If you still have access to the wallet, move your funds to a new wallet and store the new seed phrase offline.
