# Bridging between networks

## How long does a bridge transfer take?

A bridge transfer between supported networks usually takes 5 to 20 minutes.

## How much is the bridge fee?

The bridge fee is 0.05% of the amount, with a minimum fee of 1 USDT equivalent. The network (gas) fee is separate.

## My bridge transfer is stuck or still pending. What should I do?

Most bridge transfers arrive within 20 minutes. If your transfer has been pending for more than 60 minutes (one hour), report it to support and include the transaction hash. A transaction hash is public and safe to share. Your seed phrase and private key are not: support will never ask for them.
