# ACME staking

## How long does unstaking take?

ACME staking has a 7-day unstaking period. After you request to unstake, your tokens become available to withdraw 7 days later.

## When are staking rewards paid?

Staking rewards are distributed every Monday at 12:00 UTC.

## How much can I earn from staking?

Staking reward rates vary over time and are not guaranteed. Nobody can promise you a fixed return.
