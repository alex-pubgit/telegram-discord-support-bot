# Network (gas) fees

## What are network fees and who receives them?

Network fees, also called gas fees, are separate from the AcmeSwap swap fee. They go to the network that processes your transaction, not to AcmeSwap.

## Why do I need BNB, ETH or POL to swap?

Every swap is a network transaction, and you pay its gas fee in the native coin (the gas token) of that network: BNB on BNB Smart Chain, ETH on Ethereum and POL on Polygon. Keep a small amount of the native coin in your wallet, otherwise your wallet cannot send the transaction.
