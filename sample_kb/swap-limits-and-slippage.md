# Swap limits and slippage

## What is the minimum swap amount?

The minimum swap is 1 USDT equivalent. Smaller amounts cannot be swapped.

## What is slippage tolerance and how do I change it?

Slippage tolerance is the largest price change you accept between sending a swap and its confirmation. The default slippage tolerance is 0.5%. You can adjust it up to a maximum of 5%.
