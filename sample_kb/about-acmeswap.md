# About AcmeSwap

## What is AcmeSwap?

AcmeSwap is a non-custodial token swap app. You connect your own wallet and swap tokens directly from it. AcmeSwap never holds your funds.

## What does non-custodial mean?

Non-custodial means your tokens stay in your own wallet and only you control the keys. AcmeSwap never sees your seed phrase or private key. The downside is that AcmeSwap cannot recover a lost seed phrase and cannot reverse or cancel a confirmed transaction.
