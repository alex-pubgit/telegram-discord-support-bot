# Swap fees

## How much is the swap fee?

AcmeSwap charges a swap fee of 0.25% per swap. The fee is the same on every supported network: BNB Smart Chain, Ethereum and Polygon. Network (gas) fees are separate and are not included in the swap fee.

## Are stablecoin swaps cheaper?

Yes. A stablecoin-to-stablecoin swap, from one stablecoin to another, costs 0.10% instead of 0.25%.
