# Confirmed transactions

## Can a transaction be reversed, cancelled or refunded?

No. AcmeSwap is non-custodial and cannot reverse, cancel or refund a confirmed transaction. This includes a swap or a transfer sent to the wrong address. Always check the network, the token and the address before you confirm.
